This Privacy Policy explains how Berjaya AI Labs Sdn. Bhd. Berjaya AI Labs provides applied enterprise AI solutions for business operations, customer engagement, workflow automation, private AI deployment, conversational agents, document intelligence, social listening, analytics, and related technology services.

Our services may include the design, deployment, management, integration, and support of AI-powered automation systems, including WhatsApp automation solutions for businesses.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We may collect and process personal data that you provide to us directly, that is generated through your use of our website or services, or that is provided by our business customers in connection with their use of our solutions.

This may include:

Contact and identity information, such as your name, company name, job title, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, and business contact details.

Communication information, such as messages, enquiries, support requests, WhatsApp conversations, chatbot interactions, form submissions, email correspondence, call records, and other communications with us.

Business and service information, such as company requirements, project details, workflow information, system configuration details, customer support needs, and implementation records.

Technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, website usage data, cookies, log files, and analytics data.

WhatsApp Business Platform data, where applicable, such as WhatsApp user identifiers, phone numbers, message metadata, message content, opt-in and opt-out records, template message records, delivery status, and interaction history, to the extent required to provide WhatsApp automation and messaging services.

AI interaction data, such as prompts, inputs, outputs, knowledge-base queries, conversation logs, user instructions, and system usage records, where you or our customers use AI-enabled systems provided or supported by us.

We do not intentionally collect personal data that is unnecessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

3. Managing message templates, opt-ins, opt-outs, automation rules, routing, escalation, and chatbot logic.

6. Consent, Opt-In, and Opt-Out for WhatsApp Communications

Where required, we or our business customers will obtain consent before sending WhatsApp messages to users.

Consent may be obtained through website forms, checkout forms, lead forms, QR codes, sign-up forms, customer support interactions, written agreements, verbal confirmations, or other lawful opt-in methods.

You may opt out of WhatsApp messages by following the instructions provided in the message, replying with a recognised opt-out keyword such as “STOP”, or contacting the relevant business directly.

After an opt-out request is received, further non-essential WhatsApp messages should stop, except where messages are necessary for legal, security, transactional, or service-related purposes.
